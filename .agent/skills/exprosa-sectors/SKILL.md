# EXPROSA 6 Sectors Skill

Sector-specific guidelines derived from EXPROSA Sektoral PRD v1.0.

## Anti-Template & Strict Anti-AI Slop Policy
- Every client website must have a dedicated visual direction, authentic copywriting, and clear conversion funnel tailored to their sector.
- Banned AI Clichés: NO pill badges above headings, NO mid-sentence color shifts, NO generic centered sub-page banners, NO SaaS card framing for photography, NO neon glow blobs, NO floating pill buttons.
- Editorial Hospitality Standard: Asymmetric 12-column grid, sharp or micro-rounded corners (`rounded-none` or `rounded-sm` max 2px), hairline dividers (`rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), muted aged brass/bronze (`#C5A059`) & dark espresso (`#0F0D0B`), grounded sensory copywriting.

## The 6 Sectors Overview

1. **Sektor 01 — Restaurant & Food** (`PRD_SEKTOR_01_RESTAURANT_FOOD_v1.md`)
   - Goals: Dine-in discovery, menu viewing, table reservation/WhatsApp call, Google Maps directions.
   - Sitemap: Beranda, Menu, Detail Menu, Tentang, Galeri & Suasana, Lokasi/Jam Buka, Reservasi, Kontak.

2. **Sektor 02 — Catering & Event Food** (`PRD_SEKTOR_02_CATERING_EVENT_FOOD_v1.md`)
   - Goals: Inquiries for events, nasi box, wedding catering, aqiqah, corporate packages, quotation requests.
   - Sitemap: Beranda, Paket Catering, Detail Paket, Layanan Acara, Aqiqah (opsional), Galeri Acara, Area Layanan, FAQ, Kontak/Penawaran.

3. **Sektor 03 — Bakery & Custom Cake** (`PRD_SEKTOR_03_BAKERY_CUSTOM_CAKE_v1.md`)
   - Goals: Bakery catalog, custom cake order brief, pre-order lead time, delivery radius, WhatsApp consultation.
   - Sitemap: Beranda, Katalog Produk, Kategori, Detail Produk, Custom Cake, Galeri Desain, Tentang Toko, Lokasi/Kontak, FAQ Pemesanan.

4. **Sektor 04 — Coffee Shop & Roastery** (`PRD_SEKTOR_04_COFFEE_SHOP_ROASTERY_v1.md`)
   - Goals: Atmosphere discovery, beverages/beans catalog, retail/wholesale beans inquiries, maps navigation.
   - Sitemap: Beranda, Menu Kopi/Makanan, Tentang Kedai, Lokasi & Ambiance, Biji Kopi (Beans), Wholesale/B2B (opsional), Kontak.

5. **Sektor 05 — Industrial & Manufacturing** (`PRD_SEKTOR_05_INDUSTRIAL_MANUFACTURING_v1.md`)
   - Scope: Konveksi, apparel, garment, supplier, kitchen set, interior/furniture, custom manufacturing.
   - Goals: B2B quotation, production capacity proof, machinery/process transparency, client specs form.
   - Sitemap: Beranda, Produk/Kemampuan Manufaktur, Detail Produk, Fasilitas & Mesin, Alur Kerja/SOP, Portofolio Klien, Unduh Katalog, Kontak RFQ.

6. **Sektor 06 — Automotive & Technical Service** (`PRD_SEKTOR_06_AUTOMOTIVE_TECHNICAL_SERVICE_v1.md`)
   - Scope: Bengkel mobil/motor, AC mobil, kaki-kaki, racksteer, perawatan berkala.
   - Goals: Symptom checklist, WhatsApp consultation, booking service, maps location, transparent process.
   - Sitemap: Beranda, Layanan Servis, Detail Layanan, Gejala/Masalah, Fasilitas & Tools, Galeri Pengerjaan, Tentang Bengkel, Lokasi/Jam Buka, Booking/Kontak.
